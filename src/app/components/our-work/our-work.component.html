Our Work

At Ekenyoro, we are committed to fostering sustainable livelihoods and environmental stewardship through innovative apiculture and community-based projects. Our work spans various initiatives aimed at empowering communities, promoting biodiversity, and enhancing economic resilience.

Bees4Life: Climate-Smart Apiculture for Economic Resilience

The "Bees4Life: Climate-Smart Apiculture for Economic Resilience" project is a cornerstone of our efforts to integrate traditional beekeeping with modern, climate-resilient practices. This initiative aims to bolster the economic stability of smallholder farmers by diversifying their income streams through apiculture, thereby reducing their vulnerability to climate change impacts on conventional agriculture.

Project Objectives:

  • To train smallholder farmers in advanced, climate-smart beekeeping techniques.
  • To increase honey production and other bee products (e.g., beeswax, propolis) to enhance household income.
  • To promote the conservation of local bee populations and their habitats.
  • To establish market linkages for bee products, ensuring fair trade and sustainable revenue for farmers.
  • To empower women and youth by providing them with equal opportunities in apiculture.

Key Activities:

Training and Capacity Building:

Conducting workshops and hands-on training sessions on modern hive management, honey harvesting, processing, and quality control. We focus on sustainable practices that are adaptable to changing climatic conditions.

Input Support:

Beehives/Equipment Placeholder

Providing starter kits including beehives, protective gear, and essential beekeeping tools to participating farmers and groups.

Apiary Establishment and Management:

Apiary Placeholder

Assisting farmers in setting up and managing apiaries, including site selection, bee colony health monitoring, and pest/disease control using eco-friendly methods.

Value Addition and Market Access:

Honey Products Placeholder

Facilitating access to processing equipment and training on value addition (e.g., filtered honey, beeswax products). We also work on creating direct links to local and regional markets.

Monitoring and Evaluation:

Regularly monitoring project progress, collecting data on honey yields, income changes, and environmental impacts to ensure effectiveness and make necessary adjustments.

Expected Outcomes:

  • Improved economic resilience for at least 500 smallholder farming households.
  • Increased biodiversity and pollination services in project areas.
  • Enhanced community knowledge and adoption of climate-smart agricultural practices.
  • Sustainable income generation through the sale of honey and other bee products.
  • Stronger community-based organizations managing beekeeping enterprises.
